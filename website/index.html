New tab

A calmer place to land, every new tab.

Your clock, your one focus for the day, and your music, over a landmark photo that changes daily. Nothing to sign in to, and it opens instantly.

  • One focus, one click

    Set your main focus once, then press Start focusing to begin a timed session on it.

  • Tasks that stay out of the way

    A small pill that opens only when you want it. Pick a task to start a session on.

  • A dock for your sites

    Frosted-glass bookmarks and a web search that uses your own default search engine.

  • Landmark photos, quietly

    A new landmark each day. Hover the place name for its story, or tap refresh for another. Works offline.

Music

Your music, one click away.

A compact player in the corner, not a wall of controls. Tap the heart on any song and it’s the first thing you see on your next new tab.

  • Saved songs. Heart a song, an album or a whole playlist and play it again in one click.
  • Spotify. Paste a song, album or playlist link. WebWell shows the song and artist, and Next and Back work inside playlists.
  • SoundCloud. Type a song or artist name to search.
  • Offline sounds. Rain, soft noise and waves that need no internet, plus lofi radio.
  • Keeps playing after you close the tab. Pause or skip from the toolbar popup.

Spotify plays full songs when you’re signed in to Spotify in your browser, and 30-second previews when you’re not. WebWell never handles your Spotify login.

Focus sessions

Start focusing. Stay there.

Pick what you’re working on, choose a length and a sound, and the page gets out of your way. Distracting sites wait until you’re done.

  • Your length. 25, 50 or 90 minutes, or any length you like, with an automatic break afterwards.
  • Pick a task from today’s list, or type what you’re doing.
  • Calming sound. Rain, soft noise, waves or lofi radio, switchable while you work.
  • Distractions blocked. Sites you marked as distracting show a quiet “can wait” page until the timer ends.
  • See it add up. Your focused minutes show on the dashboard, with the time left on the toolbar icon.
Protect

Block what you don’t want, on your terms.

Turn on only what you need. Everything runs inside your browser, and nothing about the sites you visit is sent anywhere.

  • Adult content filter

    Blocks 47,000+ adult sites, and switches Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo to SafeSearch and YouTube to Restricted Mode.

  • Short videos

    Blocks YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Reels and Snapchat Spotlight. Regular videos still work.

  • Mindful pause

    A few slow breaths before a distracting site opens. You still decide.

  • Your own list

    Sites you add never open, until you take them off the list.

  • Parent PIN

    Protections can’t be switched off or reset without it.

  • Private by design

    The block list ships inside the extension, so the sites you visit are never sent anywhere to be checked.

A PIN can’t stop someone removing the extension in the browser’s settings. On a child’s computer, also use a supervised profile.

Privacy

Your browsing data stays on your device.

Everything WebWell tracks and stores lives in your browser. There are no servers, no analytics and no account. Blocking uses a list that ships inside the extension, so the sites you visit are never sent anywhere to be checked.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is WebWell really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no trial and no account. Everything on this page is included.

How do I install WebWell?

Open the WebWell page in the Chrome Web Store and click Add to Chrome. Brave uses the same store, so it works the same way. If you would rather install from source, follow the GitHub install guide.

Can I track my screen time in Chrome?

Yes. Chrome has no built-in screen time on desktop, so WebWell counts the time you spend on each website for you, in the background. Open the dashboard to see today's total, each site, a daily score and your busiest hours. It records only the website name and the seconds, and keeps them in your browser, with no account. Read how screen-time tracking works in Chrome.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. Your stats, settings and saved songs live in your browser. Music from Spotify, SoundCloud and SomaFM, and the AI chat, only connect to their own services when you use them.

Will WebWell block websites?

Only when you ask it to. A focus session blocks the sites you marked as distracting while it runs. The adult filter, short-video blocker and your own list work only if you turn them on. Daily time limits just send a notification.

Why does it ask for access to all sites?

Only when you switch on a blocking feature, because the browser needs that permission to redirect a blocked page. WebWell never reads or stores what’s on the pages you visit.

Can my child switch the filter off?

Set a parent PIN and the protections can’t be turned off or reset without it. A PIN can’t stop someone removing the extension from the browser’s settings, so on a child’s computer also use a supervised profile.

How does Spotify work?

Paste a link to a song, album or playlist and it plays through Spotify’s own player. If you’re signed in to Spotify in your browser you’ll hear full songs; if not, Spotify allows 30-second previews. WebWell never sees your login.

How does it count my time?

Only the active tab counts, and tracking pauses when you’re idle or the window loses focus.

What do I need for Ask WebWell AI?

Your own Gemini API key. The assistant answers only from your stats.

Which browsers does it support?

Brave and Google Chrome on desktop.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

It’s removed with the extension. Your stats only ever lived in your browser, so nothing is left anywhere else. You can export a copy from Settings first.

Is WebWell a screen time tracker and a website blocker?

Both. It tracks how long you spend on each website (only the active tab counts), scores your day, and can block distracting sites during focus sessions, or all the time if you choose to.

Is WebWell an alternative to Momentum?

WebWell is built around a similar idea: a calm new tab with a daily focus, tasks and a clock over a landmark photo. It also includes focus sessions with music, site blocking and private screen-time tracking, and it is free and open source. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with Momentum.

Does WebWell work in Edge, Firefox or Safari?

WebWell is built for Google Chrome and Brave. Other Chromium browsers such as Microsoft Edge may run it, but they aren’t officially supported yet. Firefox and Safari aren’t supported.

Does WebWell work offline?

Mostly. The new tab, landmark photos, focus timer, offline sounds and your stats all work without a connection. Spotify, SoundCloud and radio need the internet.
