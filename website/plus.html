What you get

Eleven extras, in three groups. Each one is a Plus feature in the extension, with a short note on what it does.

Stay on track

  • Block schedules

    Block distracting sites on a timetable, such as weekdays 9 to 5, or wind down after 10 pm. Set it once.

  • Strict focus mode

    Once a focus session starts, you cannot end it early, and blocked sites stay blocked until the timer ends. There is an emergency exit for real emergencies.

  • Intent check

    Before a paused site opens, or you spend earned minutes, say why you are going there. Your answers show up in your weekly report.

  • Earn your break

    Win minutes on a blocked site for each focus session you finish.

  • Focus presets

    One tap for Study, Deep work or Wind down. Each has its own timer, sound and blocked sites.

See where your time goes

  • Weekly and monthly reports

    Trends, your best and worst days, what your time really adds up to in real-life terms, and a comparison with the period before.

  • Full history, CSV and PDF

    Free keeps your last 120 days. Plus keeps every day, and lets you export your reports as CSV or print them as PDF.

  • AI weekly coach

    A short weekly plan built from your own numbers. It uses your own Google AI key, and only a short summary is sent, only when you press the button.

Make it yours

  • Sound mixer

    Layer rain, waves, brown and soft noise, cafe, fireplace, wind and crickets with your own volumes, and save your mixes. All of it is generated on your device.

  • Extra themes and backgrounds

    More backgrounds, accent colours and clock styles for your new tab.

  • Focus garden and streaks

    Every finished focus session grows your garden. Keep your streak going.
