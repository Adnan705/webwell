Is Momentum Plus worth it?

By Adnan · October 24, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: Momentum Plus costs $39.95 a year. It is worth it if you want its integrations (Todoist, Trello, Asana), your own photos or weather. If you mostly want a calm new tab with focus tools, a free option covers that.

Full disclosure: I make WebWell, the free option below. I have taken Momentum's details from its own Plus page. Prices and features change, so check it before you buy.

What Momentum Plus costs

  • $39.95 a year, billed annually (about $3.33 a month). No monthly price is listed.
  • No free trial is mentioned, but there is a 30-day money-back guarantee and you can cancel any time.
  • Over three years that is $119.85.

What Plus adds to the free version

The free Momentum is the calm dashboard: clock, photos, quotes, a daily focus and to-dos. Plus adds a site blocker, Pomodoro timers, soundscapes, countdowns, your own photos and quotes, custom colors and fonts, hourly weather, integrations with Asana, Todoist and Trello, notes with AI, tab groups and world clocks.

Is it worth it?

Yes, if you live in Todoist, Trello or Asana and want tasks on your new tab, or you want your own photos, weather and countdowns, and you like the polish.

Probably not, if you mainly want a calm page, a focus timer, sounds and a way to keep distracting sites away. You can get those without paying.

Its pricing page does not mention a free trial. It does offer a 30-day money-back guarantee and says you can cancel any time.
