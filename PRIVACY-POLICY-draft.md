# WebWell: Privacy Policy (plain-text copy)

_Last updated: 2026-10-30. Applies to WebWell 1.9.0 and the website webwell.app. The published version is https://webwell.app/privacy; if the two differ, the website is the one that counts._

## Who makes WebWell
WebWell is a free, open-source browser extension for Google Chrome and Brave, made by Adnan Khan, an independent developer. It is not run by a company and it has no servers that receive your browsing data. The source code is public: https://github.com/Adnan705/webwell

## What WebWell does
WebWell is a calm new tab with a focus timer, music, site blocking and private screen-time tracking. WebWell Plus is an optional paid upgrade that adds more features.

## Data collection and storage
- WebWell records the domain of your active tab and the time spent on it. It does not store full URLs, page titles, page content or keystrokes.
- **Everything is stored on your device**, in your browser's local extension storage. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no third-party trackers in the extension.
- Blocking runs inside your browser using Chrome's built-in blocking rules. The adult-site list ships with the extension; WebWell never sends the sites you visit anywhere to check them.
- WebWell counts how many pages it blocked each day so it can show you the number. That count stays on your device.
- Site icons come from your browser's own icon cache. The fonts are bundled with the extension.

## Features that use the network (only when you use them)
- **Ask WebWell (AI chat, optional):** only works with your own Gemini API key. When you ask a question, your question and a short summary of your statistics are sent to Google's Gemini API. Nothing is sent at any other time. Your key is stored only in your browser.
- **AI weekly coach (WebWell Plus, optional):** only works with your own Google AI key. When you press the button, a short summary of your own numbers is sent to Google. Nothing is sent at any other time.
- **Lofi radio:** streams music from SomaFM when you press play.
- **SoundCloud:** searches and plays tracks from SoundCloud when you use that tab.
- **Spotify:** when you play something from the Spotify tab, WebWell loads Spotify's own embedded player (open.spotify.com) and asks Spotify's public pages for the names, artists and covers of the songs in the link you pasted. Spotify's privacy policy applies to that player. WebWell never sees your Spotify login and has no Spotify account access.
- The rain, soft-noise and wave sounds are generated on your device and need no network.
- **Saved songs** (the heart button) and pages you save to your new tab are stored only on your device.
- **Search box:** a search on the new tab goes to your browser's default search engine.

## WebWell Plus
- **Keys.** A Plus key is checked inside the extension against a public key that ships with the code. No server is contacted, and nothing about you or your key is sent to anyone. The key is saved in your browser's local extension storage. The maker keeps a private list of the keys issued, with only a label and a key ID. It never includes browsing data, because the maker never receives any.
- **Buying.** Payments are handled by Paddle, which is the seller of record. Paddle loads its own checkout on webwell.app/plus, and Paddle's privacy notice applies to what you enter there. The maker never sees your card details. After you pay, Paddle gives the maker your name, email address and order number, which are used only to email your key, answer questions and handle a refund, and are kept with your key so it can be sent again if you lose it. None of this involves your browsing data.

## Permissions
When you install WebWell, your browser shows one line: change the page you see when you open a new tab. Everything else is asked for only when you switch the feature on, and you can say no.

Required (granted at install):
- **Storage:** to keep your stats and settings on your device.
- **Idle, alarms:** to pause when you step away and to run the focus timer.
- **Offscreen:** to keep music playing after you close the new tab.
- **Search:** so the new tab's search box uses your browser's default search engine.
- **Active tab:** used only by the popup's "Save this page" button, when you press it.
- **Scripting and declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess:** to block sites and hide short-video feeds using the browser's own rules, on sites you have allowed.

Optional (asked when you use the feature):
- **Tabs, web navigation, site icons:** to know which site is active, count screen time and show the right message on the blocked page. Without them WebWell cannot count your time.
- **Notifications:** limit alerts and focus reminders.
- **Access to all sites:** only if you switch on blocking or short-video hiding.
- **SoundCloud and Spotify:** only when you play from them.
- **Google AI:** only when you use the optional AI chat or the Plus weekly coach with your own key.

## This website
- Google Analytics runs only if you accept the cookie banner. If you decline, nothing from Google loads.
- The goodbye page (opened after you remove the extension) is optional. If you send it, only what you type is received, delivered by Formspree.
- The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs.

## Data deletion
- Uninstalling the extension deletes all recorded data immediately, since it only ever existed in your browser.
- You can export, import or reset your data from WebWell's Settings at any time.
