# WebWell — Privacy Policy (draft for public launch)

_Last updated: 2026-10-17_

## What WebWell does
WebWell is a browser extension that shows you how you spend your time on the web: which websites you visit, how long you spend on each, and whether that time is productive or distracting.

## Data collection and storage
- WebWell records your browsing activity (domains visited and time spent on the active tab) **entirely on your device**, inside your browser's local storage.
- **Your tracking data never leaves your browser.** There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.
- The extension observes which tab is active in order to count time per website. This information is processed locally and is never transmitted anywhere.
- Optional AI features (the Ask AI chat): these only work if you add your own Gemini API key in Settings. When you ask a question, your question plus a short summary of your statistics is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the answer. Nothing is sent at any other time, and your API key is stored only in your browser.
- Minor network use: the dashboard loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, and site icons are fetched from Google's favicon service. These requests contain no personal data.
- WebWell does not set cookies.

## Data deletion
- Uninstalling the extension deletes all recorded data immediately, since it only ever existed in your browser's local storage.
- You can also clear your history from the extension's Settings tab at any time.

## Your data belongs to you
- There is no signup, no cloud sync, and no one else has access to your statistics.
