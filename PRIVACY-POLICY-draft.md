# WebWell — Privacy Policy (draft for public launch)

_Last updated: 2026-10-20_

## What WebWell does
WebWell is a browser extension that shows you how you spend your time on the web, helps you focus with a timer ("Focus sessions"), and can block adult sites, short-video feeds and sites you choose.

## Data collection and storage
- WebWell records the domain of your active tab and the time spent on it. It does not store full URLs, page titles, page content or keystrokes.
- **Everything is stored on your device**, in your browser's local storage. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no third-party trackers.
- Blocking runs inside your browser using Chrome's built-in blocking rules. The adult-site list ships with the extension; WebWell never sends the sites you visit anywhere to check them.
- WebWell counts how many pages it blocked each day so it can show you the number. That count stays on your device.
- Site icons come from your browser's own icon cache. The fonts are bundled with the extension.

## Features that use the network (only when you use them)
- **Ask WebWell (AI chat):** only works with your own Gemini API key. When you ask a question, your question and a short summary of your statistics are sent to Google's Gemini API. Nothing is sent at any other time. Your key is stored only in your browser.
- **Lofi radio:** streams music from SomaFM when you press play.
- **SoundCloud:** searches and plays tracks from SoundCloud when you use that tab.
- **Spotify:** when you play something from the Spotify tab, WebWell loads Spotify's own embedded player (open.spotify.com) and asks Spotify's public pages for the names, artists and covers of the songs in the link you pasted. Spotify's privacy policy applies to that player; whether you hear full songs or 30-second previews depends on whether you are signed in to Spotify in your browser. WebWell never sees your Spotify login and has no Spotify account access.
- The rain, soft-noise and wave sounds are generated on your device and need no network.
- **Saved songs** (the heart button) are stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage.

## Permissions
- **Tabs, idle, alarms, web navigation:** to know which site is active, pause when you step away, run the focus timer and show the right message on the blocked page.
- **Storage:** to keep your stats and settings on your device.
- **Notifications:** for time-limit alerts and focus-session reminders.
- **Offscreen:** to keep music playing after you close the new tab.
- **Declarative net request, scripting, access to all sites (optional):** to block sites and hide short-video feeds. WebWell only asks for site access when you turn on a blocking feature.
- **Search:** so the new tab's search box uses your browser's default search engine.

## Data deletion
- Uninstalling the extension deletes all recorded data immediately, since it only ever existed in your browser.
- You can delete your history or reset WebWell from its Settings at any time.
