I build WebWell on my own: a free, open-source browser extension for Chrome and Brave. It gives you a calm new tab, a focus timer, site blocking and screen-time tracking that stays on your device.

Today I am launching an optional upgrade called WebWell Plus. I want to be clear about how it works, because it matters to me.

Everything that is free today stays free. Plus only adds. It costs $10 once, with no subscription and no account.

Plus adds block schedules, a strict focus mode, an intent check before you open a blocked site, weekly and monthly reports, full history with CSV export, focus presets, a sound mixer, extra themes, a focus garden, and an AI weekly coach that uses your own Google AI key.

You unlock it with a key that the extension checks offline. No server is contacted and nothing is sent anywhere, so the privacy promise of the free version does not change.

Why charge at all? Because I want to keep maintaining this as a small independent project, and a one-time price felt more honest to me than ads, tracking or a subscription.
